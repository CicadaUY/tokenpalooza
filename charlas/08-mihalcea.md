# Charla 8 — Collective Wisdom and Collective Failure in Multi-Agent Systems

- **Expositor/a:** Rada Mihalcea
- **Cuándo:** martes 3 de noviembre, 14:30

## Resumen

When many AI agents interact, they begin to show behaviors we usually think of as distinctly human: although they lack the consciousness, emotions, and social intuition of human societies, they inherit some of our collective weaknesses and also demonstrate some of our collective strengths. For instance, agents can converge on group beliefs regardless of evidence, they can amplify implicit biases through their interactions, and groups of agents can collapse by overusing their shared resources. At the same time, multi-agent systems also offer the power of many: diverse agents that deliberate, critique, and build on one another can reason more robustly than any single model. In this talk, I will explore both the failures and the strengths of multi-agent systems, drawing on large-scale generative agent experiments, studies on bias in multi-agent interactions, and insights into misinformation and group behavior. I will also discuss the broader implications of these systems – not only for the future of AI but also for human-centered disciplines such as psychology, sociology, and ethics, where they can challenge or facilitate our understanding of intelligence, agency, and collective decision-making.
